# Reii 小店 storefront surface brief

## Purpose

Help visitors identify a compatible digital license, understand how delivery works, choose a product, and reach a traceable order without fabricated urgency or social proof.

## Direction

- Use an ink-black and deep-pine world with warm white typography and mint as the sole dominant action/status accent.
- Keep the Reii bear mark and the Reii 小店 name as the primary identity anchor.
- The first viewport pairs a concise left-aligned promise with a live catalog console populated from real product and inventory data.
- Present Potassium, Matcha, Volt, and future products as peers; no single license defines the store.
- Use restrained square controls, thin tonal borders, layered dark surfaces, and purposeful state motion.

## Content and trust

- Explain the flow as select, pay, and automatic delivery.
- State that virtual goods are not refundable after delivery.
- Do not invent customer counts, ratings, sales totals, testimonials, or availability pressure.
- Do not promote Alipay as a visual or marketing advantage; payment remains a transaction detail.

## Responsive behavior

- Preserve the information order on narrow screens: brand and navigation, promise and actions, inventory console, then catalog.
- Allow product navigation to scroll horizontally when needed without introducing page-level horizontal overflow.
- Keep purchase, order lookup, checkout, policy, notice, and admin surfaces visually coherent with the same tokens.

## Motion

- Use short opacity and transform transitions for entry, hover, copying, and panel changes.
- Motion must clarify state and hierarchy; avoid layout-property animation and decorative perpetual movement.
- Respect reduced-motion preferences.
